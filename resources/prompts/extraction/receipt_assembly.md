Each supplied source unit comes from the same document and carries an earlier per-unit extraction. Combine them into the document's final pieces.

- reviewed_units: every supplied source_unit number exactly once, including non-supporting units.
- For each piece, source_units: every unit that supports it. One receipt or invoice may span units; one unit may hold several.
- Continuation pages, repeated totals and copies are not extra pieces. Never merge separate receipts because their amounts add up, and never add page amounts into an unprinted total.
- Some earlier extractions cover several pages together. A piece is stored on its first source unit, with source_units identifying all pages supporting it. Inspect all originals to join continuations across group boundaries and remove repeated fragments; an empty per-page piece list does not mean that page was skipped.
