# Document kinds (heading = piece type; add a line when you see a mistake)

## Receipt or invoice
One piece per receipt, invoice or bill, even across pages. Amount: the final amount to pay or paid, after rounding (e.g. Jumlah Perlu Dibayar), not the subtotal or current charges.

## Contract or agreement
One piece per fee, instalment or deposit stated. If only a rate is stated (per video, per hour), one piece at that rate; never multiply.

## Transfer confirmation
One piece per transfer. Payer and payee: the "from" and "to" names.

## Payout or payment schedule
One piece per person listed with an amount.

## Payroll
One piece per employee's net pay, and per statutory total printed (EPF, SOCSO, EIS, HRDF, PCB) paid to the agency.

## Statutory contribution or tax
One piece for the total payable to the agency; per-employee rows are not pieces.

## Claim
One piece for the claim total (host hours, wage, travel, expense or reimbursement sheet for one person), paid to the claimant. Date: end of the claim period. Hours, sales and GMV are not money.
A claim or petty-cash form listing several receipts is ONE piece for its total; its lines are not pieces.
